Clankers

Player 1 uses ←→ to run, Z or Space to jump (A), X for the special (B), ↓ to crouch or slide (or to drop into a hatch) and Enter for Start. On a phone, use the touch buttons.

Player 2 uses AD to run, G to jump, H for the special, S to crouch. Press G on the player screen to join. USB or Bluetooth game controllers also work, one per player.

This build is all of World 1: three levels, the Bolt Shower bonus and the Clanky Crane. Little mode slows the Glitchies and the crane down. Progress saves in this browser.
